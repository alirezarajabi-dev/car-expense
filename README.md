# Car Expense Log

A phone app for the monthly car expense form: daily odometer readings, parking receipts and the month-end fuel price, exported as a printable Letter-size PDF. English, French and Persian.

Open the app: **https://alirezarajabi-dev.github.io/car-expense/**

## Install on your phone
- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome, tap ⋮, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

No account is needed. Your log and receipt photos are saved on your phone only, and the app works without a signal.
Use **Month settings → Save backup** now and then to keep a copy or move it to a new phone.

## Every month
1. Each working day: enter the start and end odometer; the distance is calculated. Add parking receipts (amount + photo).
2. At month end: enter the fuel price per 100 km and any overtime.
3. Tap **Print form (PDF)**, then print it or send it.
